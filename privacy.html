Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

PickClip Studio is designed to minimize the collection of personal information and to use a local-first desktop workflow whenever practical.

Information used by the service

When a user chooses to connect a supported third-party service, PickClip Studio may process information made available through that service's authorization system. This may include a basic account identifier, profile information permitted by the user, authorization tokens, and information needed to submit or monitor content selected by the user.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide requested features such as account connection, content review, publishing approved content, and checking the status of an approved publishing request.

Local storage

The desktop version uses a local-first design. Local project files and configuration data may be stored on the user's own computer. Where account authorization is enabled, authorization tokens may also be stored locally so that the requested integration can operate.

Sharing of information

PickClip Studio does not sell personal information. Information is shared with a connected third-party service only when necessary to provide a feature requested by the user.

Third-party services

Connected services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the policies of each service they choose to connect.

Data retention and control

Users may disconnect connected services and may delete locally stored PickClip Studio configuration data from their device. Third-party permissions may also be revoked through the relevant third-party account settings.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce unnecessary exposure of account authorization information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

PickClip Studio is not directed to children and is intended for users who are permitted to use the connected services under their applicable terms.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as PickClip Studio changes. The effective date on this page will be updated when material changes are made.
